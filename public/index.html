What gets checked

Consistency Form against the client account record and the delivering firm statement, including name, SSN, registration and account number.
Completeness Signature, signature date, Medallion guarantee where the value requires one, and a transfer election with both a type and a scope.
Contra firm fit The right form and entity for the delivering firm, and an account number in the format that firm uses.

Issues are reported with the standard reason codes, NIGO-01 through NIGO-10. The workflow advises, a reviewer decides, and corrections are made by the advisor.
